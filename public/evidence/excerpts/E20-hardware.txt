The event callback calls the inherited
`dispatchKeyboardEvent` with Consumer page `0x0c`: usage `0x30` for Power
(the iPod hold/sleep-wake button) and usage `0x40` for either Volume key
(the iPod menu/Home button). These usages and the user-client path were
checked against the original 4A102 binary and SpringBoard's HID filter.

The LRADC at `0x01c21800` is polled with its IRQ disabled.
The live Jumpdrive tree declares Volume Up at 200 mV and Down at 400 mV on
channel 0, supplied by the 3 V ALDO3 rail. The A64's two-thirds reference
gives expected six-bit codes near 6 and 13, with bounded ranges on either
side of their midpoint.
