The earliest official Apple XNU tag found in this survey with both real `osfmk/arm` and `pexpert/arm` implementation trees is [`xnu-4570.1.46`](https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/xnu/tree/xnu-4570.1.46), commit `76e12aa3ea3036173a61fa1081c3be890e626e79`. It includes [`config/MASTER.arm`](https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/xnu/blob/xnu-4570.1.46/config/MASTER.arm), [`osfmk/conf/files.arm`](https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/xnu/blob/xnu-4570.1.46/osfmk/conf/files.arm), [`pexpert/conf/files.arm`](https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/xnu/blob/xnu-4570.1.46/pexpert/conf/files.arm), and the ARM make fragments. This is a genuine ARM32 source/build structure, but much later than 933.
