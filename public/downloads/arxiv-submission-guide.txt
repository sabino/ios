ARXIV SUBMISSION GUIDE - 7 OCTOBER 2026

Status: preprint prepared for submission; not yet submitted or peer reviewed.

1. Finish your arXiv account registration and author identity. If prompted,
   complete endorsement for the selected category.
2. Start a new submission. Suggested primary category: cs.OS (Operating Systems).
   This is a recommendation based on the subject, not an arXiv decision.
3. Choose the license yourself. The repository MIT license covers original
   material; depicted third-party interfaces retain their original rights.
4. Upload early-iphone-os-native-pinephone-preprint-source.zip.
   Do not upload the reader PDF instead of the TeX source. The archive includes
   main.tex, every used figure and curated supporting data in anc/.
5. Select PDFLaTeX with TeX Live 2025, with main.tex as the top-level file.
   No shell escape or automatic image conversion is needed.
6. Use the accompanying metadata JSON to paste the exact title, author,
   abstract and comments. Journal reference, DOI and report number are blank.
   Add an affiliation or ORCID only if you choose to supply verified details.
7. Inspect the PDF generated by arXiv: title/author, all pages, seven figures,
   ten tables, fifteen references, internal E-record links and readable fonts.
   Compare with the supplied reader PDF. Confirm the ancillary files are listed.
8. Review the final submission and complete the portal's author attestations.
   No submission, license selection or account action has been performed here.
